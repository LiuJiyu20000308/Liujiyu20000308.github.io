About Me

I am a direct-track doctoral student in Computational Mathematics at Zhejiang University. My work spans production database systems and high-order numerical computing. My doctoral research focuses on fourth-order finite-volume and multigrid methods for elliptic and incompressible-flow problems on complex geometries. I am interested in AI infrastructure, high-performance C++ systems, and quantitative infrastructure.
